    Coupling tissue morphology to gene programs across human cancers

    First Author1, Second Author1, Third Author2, Corresponding Author1,*

    1 Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China · 2 Collaborating Institute, City, Country

    Paper Code Data Cite

    Abstract

    Histological morphology and molecular state are two complementary readouts of a tumour, yet how specific tissue patterns couple to gene programs remains poorly mapped. Here we learn a morphology–molecule coupling (MMC) network from paired whole-slide images and transcriptomic profiles. Each morphological node is one of the 1,024 UNI dimensions, labelled by its index (MRM_427 is UNI dimension 427), and each gene node is a morphology-associated gene (MPG). Spectral clustering organises the joint network into refined modules, which we annotate with Hallmark gene sets. This explorer provides the learned network, MPGs and MRMs for every organ.
